Ad provider note:
The app tracks and rewards completed ad sessions. For a real Monetag/Adsgram ad to open, the provider's official SDK/script must already be loaded. Monetag unit IDs are supported through show_<unit_id>; Adsgram is supported through Adsgram.init({blockId: unit_id}).
